

### Role guidance and tool privileges

Correcting a role prompt does not authorize new tools or task replay. Preserve active peer work and original histories; distinguish a bounded model-behavior mitigation from a structural parser repair.


### Resource-guard diagnosis

Preserve guard behavior until the initiating failure is attributable. Diagnostic cleanup must own only its observer lifecycle; dropped evidence, identity uncertainty and failed terminal capture remain inconclusive. Clean calls establish bounded operation, not permanent reliability. [Review lesson](case-studies/failure-evidence-before-guard-changes.md).
